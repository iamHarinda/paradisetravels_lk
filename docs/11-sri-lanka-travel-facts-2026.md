## 1. Visa / ETA (high-value, high-search topic)
- From **25 May 2026**, nationals of **40 countries** can get a Sri Lanka **tourist ETA free of charge**, valid **30 days** with **double entry** from first arrival. Source: Department of Immigration & Emigration notice (immigration.gov.lk) and ETA portal.
- The free scheme covers diplomatic, official, service and ordinary passports of those countries.
- **An ETA is still mandatory** for all foreign nationals (including the 40 countries and reciprocal-agreement countries: Maldives, Seychelles, Singapore) before arrival. Apply only via the official portal **eta.gov.lk**.
- Maldivian nationals receive 90 days under a bilateral agreement.
- Staying beyond 30 days → apply for an extension in Sri Lanka and pay the applicable fee.
- Fees paid before 25 May 2026 are non-refundable.
- Countries covered include the UK, USA, India, China, Canada, Australia and most of the EU (get the exact list of 40 from the official notice and publish it as a table).
- Content idea: "Sri Lanka ETA 2026 — is it really free? Step-by-step" + "We help with your ETA" service angle. Warn readers about unofficial paid ETA sites.

## 2. Tourist arrivals 2026 (SLTDA data, via news reports)
- **1,678,745** international arrivals from 1 January to 27 September 2026; 143,623 in 1–27 September.
- January–August 2026: 1,535,122 arrivals, about **2% lower** than the same period in 2025.
- Monthly records: **277,327 in January 2026** and **279,328 in February 2026** — the highest monthly arrivals on record.
- Top markets Jan–Sep 2026: **India** (~431k to 27 Sep — about 30% share in September), **UK** (~158k to ~21 Sep), **China** (~111k, growing ~10.7%), **Germany** (~97k), **Australia** (~86k, growing ~11.4%). Russia also a major market early in the year.

## 3. Seasons (stable knowledge — still review)
- **West & south coasts + hill country:** best roughly **December–April** (south-west monsoon brings rain approx. May–September).
- **East coast (Trincomalee, Arugam Bay, Pasikudah):** best roughly **April/May–September**.
- **Cultural Triangle:** good much of the year; hottest Mar–May; north-east monsoon rain approx. Oct–Jan.
- Arugam Bay surf season roughly May–October. Mirissa whale watching roughly Nov–Apr; Trincomalee roughly May–Oct.
- Kandy Esala Perahera: July/August (exact dates follow the lunar calendar — publish each year from official announcements).
- Sri Lanka is "always in season somewhere" — use this as a key message and power the Season Picker component.

## 4. Practical facts to include (verify before use)
- Main airport: Bandaranaike International Airport (CMB), Katunayake, ~30–35 km north of Colombo.
- Currency: Sri Lankan Rupee (LKR). Cards accepted widely in cities/hotels; cash for small shops/tuk-tuks.
- Temples: cover shoulders and knees, remove shoes and hats; don't pose with back to Buddha statues.
- Poya (full-moon) days are public holidays; alcohol sales are restricted.
- Driving is on the left; most visitors hire a car with a driver.
- Emergency numbers: Police 119, Tourist Police (check current number), ambulance 1990 (Suwa Seriya).
