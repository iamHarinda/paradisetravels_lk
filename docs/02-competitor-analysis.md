# 02 — Competitor Analysis

Goal: understand what the named competitors do well, where they are weak, and how Paradise Travels can stand out. Re-check competitor sites before final design sign-off — they change.

## Summary matrix

| Competitor | Positioning | Strengths | Weaknesses / gaps to exploit |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Jetwing Travels** (jetwingtravels.com) | "No.1 tour agency", 40+ years, DMC, part of Jetwing hotel family | Huge itinerary library by duration (5/8/10-day pages), themed collections (Authentic Ceylon, Barefoot Luxury, Following the Wild, Island of Wellness), "Plan your trip" multi-step builder, video-call booking | Corporate and hotel-group bias (pushes own Jetwing hotels); premium pricing; generic copy; some negative reviews on after-sales follow-up |
| **Aitken Spence Travels** | Large conglomerate DMC, inbound leisure + MICE | Brand trust, MICE credibility, big partner network | Corporate feel, less personal, slower to respond to small travellers |
| **2nd Chance Travels** (2ndchance.travel) | Est. 1999, branch network in Sri Lanka + Italy, Australia, UAE; strong in air ticketing | Clear growth story ("one office in 1999, four countries today"), multi-country phones, Tripadvisor listing with reviews | Air-ticket first; inbound tourism content is thin |
| **Metro Travels, Classic Travels, Jord Squared Travels, Saint Andrew Travels** | Mid-size local agencies, mostly ticketing + tours | Local trust, price | Typically dated websites, little SEO content, weak English storytelling |

## What the leaders do that we must match
1. **Itineraries by duration** — users search "Sri Lanka 7 day itinerary", "10 day Sri Lanka tour". Jetwing has a page per duration. → We build 3, 5, 7, 10, 14, 21-day pages.
2. **Themed collections** — honeymoon, wildlife, wellness, culture, adventure, family, luxury, budget.
3. **Trip-planner form** — multi-step: regions → travel style → interests → dates/budget → contact.
4. **Human contact** — named specialist, WhatsApp, video call.
5. **Social proof** — reviews visible on the site and on Tripadvisor/Google.

## Where Paradise Travels can win
1. **"All-in-one" proposition** — none of the competitors make a clean story out of *flights + visa + hotels + transport + guides + interpreters + events + Ayurveda* under one roof. This is our hero message.
2. **Exclusive hotel rates** — a dedicated "Hotel Deals" section with partner rates (see doc 10). Competitors push packages; we let travellers book just a hotel or just a car too.
3. **Speed & design** — competitor sites are heavy. A 2030-grade, sub-2-second site is itself a differentiator (and a ranking factor via Core Web Vitals).
4. **Content depth** — a genuinely useful, up-to-date Sri Lanka travel guide hub (ETA 2026, seasons by coast, train routes, costs). Competitors have little fresh guide content.
5. **Korea niche** — KakaoTalk + Korea history. Korean-language pages face little competition.
6. **Personal leadership story** — Chairman/MD visible, family-run feel versus conglomerates.
7. **MICE for small–mid groups** — conferences, workshops, day outings for groups that are too small for Aitken Spence's attention.

## Keyword reality check
Competing head-on for "Sri Lanka tours" against Jetwing and big OTAs takes time. Strategy (detail in doc 06):
- Year 1: win long-tail ("Sri Lanka 7 day itinerary with Ayurveda", "Kandy to Ella train tickets", "Sri Lanka ETA free 2026 how to apply", "Nugegoda travel agency", "travel agent near Colombo for Korea flights").
- Build topical authority with 100+ guide pages, then push for head terms.
