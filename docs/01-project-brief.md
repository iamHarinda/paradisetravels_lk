## 2. Problems with the current site / presence
1. **Single-page site**, nav items "About Us" and "Blog" point to `#` — almost nothing for Google to rank.
2. Service images are repeated placeholders (`service_1/2/3.png`), services have one-line descriptions, no service pages.
3. No packages, itineraries, prices, hotel offers, reviews, or trust signals (licences, years, partners).
4. Meta description targets "discount airline tickets" only — misses the inbound tourism market the owner now wants.
5. `og:image`, `og:url`, `twitter:image` are empty → poor social sharing previews.
6. Footer says © 2023 — looks unmaintained.
7. Tripadvisor listing: **zero reviews**, categorised under **"Nightlife"**, shows opening hours of 12:00 AM–11:30 PM. Needs correcting (category → Travel Agencies / Tours; real hours; photos).
8. Destination section mislabels (e.g. Nuwara Eliya listed twice; "Kandy — Remember the brave martyrs" is unclear to foreigners).

## 3. Goals
1. Become visible to **foreigners searching for Sri Lanka travel** on Google, Bing, and AI assistants (ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot, Perplexity).
2. Present the company as an **"all-in-one" travel partner** — the owner's own feedback: visiting the page should feel like everything is in one place.
3. **Sell discounted hotel rates** and packages; capture qualified enquiries.
4. Compete in search and perception with Jetwing Travels, Aitken Spence Travels, 2nd Chance Travels, Metro Travels, Classic Travels, Jord Squared Travels, Saint Andrew Travels.
5. Look like a 2030-ready premium brand: animated, immersive, but **extremely fast**.

## 5. Audience personas
1. **Emma, 34, UK** — first trip to Sri Lanka, 14 days, wants culture + beaches + safari, worries about safety, visa, getting around. Researches for weeks, reads guides.
2. **Rahul & Priya, 30s, India** — short 5–6-day trip (Ramayana trail, Kandy, Nuwara Eliya, beach). Price-sensitive, WhatsApp-first.
3. **Klaus & Ingrid, 60s, Germany** — 3-week Ayurveda + round trip. Wants quality, detail, reliability, German-language support is a big plus.
4. **Ji-ho, 28, South Korea** — honeymoon or friends' trip; KakaoTalk preferred.
5. **Corporate planner (local or foreign)** — conference/incentive/event in Sri Lanka; needs a proposal fast.
6. **Sri Lankan outbound traveller / worker** — needs air tickets + visa. Keep a clear path for them, but the homepage hero targets foreigners.
