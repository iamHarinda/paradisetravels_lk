# 05 — Content Strategy

## 1. Brand voice
- **Warm, knowledgeable, honest.** A local friend who happens to be a travel expert.
- Clear British/International English (most international readers are UK, AU, EU, IN).
- Short sentences, concrete details (times, distances, months), no clichés like "hidden gem", "nestled", "breathtaking" in every line.
- Honest about trade-offs (monsoons, travel times, crowds). Honesty builds trust and ranks better.

## 7. Content rules
- No AI-spun fluff. Every article must contain specifics a local expert would know (owner/team review before publishing).
- Facts with dates/numbers → source link + last-checked date.
- Unique copy on every page; no duplicated destination text.
- Reviews: only real, with permission; show source and date; never edit wording beyond trimming.
- Localisation later: translate top 20 pages per language with human review — no raw machine translation published.

## 8. Content production cadence
- Launch: home, 12 services, 15 destinations, 8 experiences, 8 tours, 15 hotel pages, 10 guides, about, contact, legal.
- After launch: 8 guides/month + 2 new tours/month + monthly "Sri Lanka travel news" update.
