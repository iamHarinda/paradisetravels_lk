## 3. Review engine (most important off-page lever)
1. After every trip/service, send a thank-you WhatsApp + email (template) with direct links: Google review link (short URL `g.page/r/...`), Tripadvisor review link.
2. Ask during the high point (last evening of tour), via guide/driver with a QR card in the vehicle.
3. Reply to every review within 48h (owner/manager name).
4. Never buy, incentivise, or write reviews — platforms penalise this and it's illegal in many markets.
5. Show reviews on site (`/reviews` + home wall) with permission; link out to source.
6. Target: 10 Google reviews/month.

## 4. Backlinks & PR
- **Data asset:** "Sri Lanka Tourism Statistics 2026" page updated monthly from SLTDA data — journalists and bloggers link to it.
- **Guides worth linking:** the ETA 2026 guide, best time to visit, train guide.
- **Partner hotels:** ask each partner hotel to link to us as a booking partner; we link to them.
- **Press:** pitch the "all-in-one since 2007" story and the Korea connection to Daily FT, Daily News, Sunday Times business, EconomyNext, travel trade media (e.g. Travel And Tour World). Add a /about/press page.
- **Guest content:** travel bloggers/YouTubers — hosted trips in exchange for honest coverage (disclosed).
- **HARO-style platforms / expert quotes** for Sri Lanka travel questions.
- **Avoid:** paid link schemes, PBNs, spammy directories.

## 5. Social media plan (feeds the site)

- YouTube: 2 videos/month (itinerary walkthroughs, hotel tours).
- Every post links to a specific page with UTM, not just the homepage.
- Embed latest Instagram posts on site only via static build-time fetch (no heavy widget).
