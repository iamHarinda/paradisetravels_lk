**Coming to Sri Lanka?** Every foreign visitor needs an Electronic Travel Authorisation (ETA) before arrival. From 25 May 2026, the tourist ETA is free for nationals of 40 countries — but it is still mandatory, and unofficial websites still charge for it. We point you to the official portal and help if anything is unclear. Read our full [Sri Lanka ETA guide](/travel-guide/sri-lanka-visa-eta).

**Travelling from Sri Lanka?** Visa rules differ by country and change often. We help you understand the requirements, prepare documents and avoid the mistakes that cause delays.
