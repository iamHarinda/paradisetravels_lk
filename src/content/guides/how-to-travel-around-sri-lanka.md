## Car with driver — the most popular option
Driving is on the left and roads can be busy and slow, so most visitors hire a **car with a driver** rather than self-driving. It is flexible, comfortable and lets you stop wherever you like. See [transport & chauffeur](/services/transport-chauffeur).

## The train — for the views
The hill-country line between **Kandy and Ella** is one of the world’s most scenic train journeys. It is slow, and reserved seats sell out, so book ahead. A popular approach: take the train for this section while your driver takes the luggage by road. Read more on [scenic train journeys](/experiences/scenic-train-journeys).

## Distances take longer than they look
Sri Lanka is small, but average speeds are low outside the expressways. Plan for roughly 3–4 hours of driving on most days of a round trip.

## Arriving
The main airport is **Bandaranaike International Airport (CMB)** at Katunayake, roughly 30–35 km north of Colombo. Negombo is the closest beach town.
