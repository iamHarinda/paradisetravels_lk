**The short answer:** from **25 May 2026**, nationals of **40 countries** can get a Sri Lanka tourist ETA **free of charge**. But an ETA is still **mandatory** for every foreign visitor — free does not mean "no ETA needed".

## What is the Sri Lanka ETA?

An Electronic Travel Authorisation (ETA) is permission to travel to Sri Lanka that you obtain online before your trip. All foreign nationals need one before arrival, including nationals of countries covered by the free scheme.

## What changed on 25 May 2026

|                   |                                            |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| Who               | Nationals of 40 countries                  |
| Cost              | Free of charge                             |
| Validity          | 30 days, double entry, from first arrival  |
| Passports covered | Diplomatic, official, service and ordinary |
| Still required?   | Yes — apply before you travel              |

**The 40 countries:** Australia, Austria, Bahrain, Belarus, Belgium, Canada, China, Czech Republic, Denmark, Finland, France, Germany, India, Indonesia, Iran, Israel, Italy, Japan, Kazakhstan, Kuwait, Malaysia, Nepal, Netherlands, New Zealand, Norway, Oman, Pakistan, Poland, Qatar, Russia, Saudi Arabia, South Korea, Spain, Sweden, Switzerland, Thailand, Turkey, the United Arab Emirates, the United Kingdom and the United States.

Nationals of the Maldives, Seychelles and Singapore were already processed free of charge and are not part of the 40. Always confirm your nationality on the official portal, [eta.gov.lk](https://eta.gov.lk), before you apply.

Fees paid **before** 25 May 2026 are not refundable.

## Special cases

- **Maldives:** Maldivian nationals receive 90 days under a bilateral agreement.
- **Reciprocal-agreement countries** (Maldives, Seychelles, Singapore) still need an ETA before arrival.
- **Staying longer than 30 days:** apply for an extension in Sri Lanka and pay the applicable fee.

## How to apply

1. Go to the **official** portal: [eta.gov.lk](https://eta.gov.lk).
2. Fill in your passport and travel details.
3. Submit and wait for approval before you fly.

## Watch out for unofficial ETA sites

Several commercial websites look official and charge a "service fee" for an ETA that may be free for you. Always type **eta.gov.lk** yourself rather than clicking an advert.
