_Figures from SLTDA data as reported in the news sources below. Re-checked monthly._

## Arrivals in 2026

| Period                        | International arrivals                              |
| ----------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| 1 January – 27 September 2026 | **1,678,745**                                       |
| 1–27 September 2026           | 143,623                                             |
| January–August 2026           | 1,535,122 (about 2% lower than January–August 2025) |

## Record months

- **January 2026:** 277,327 arrivals
- **February 2026:** 279,328 arrivals — the highest monthly figure on record

## Top source markets (January–September 2026, approximate)

| Market         | Arrivals              | Note                            |
| -------------- | --------------------- | ------------------------------- |
| India          | ~431,000 (to 27 Sep)  | About 30% of September arrivals |
| United Kingdom | ~158,000 (to ~21 Sep) |                                 |
| China          | ~111,000              | Growing ~10.7%                  |
| Germany        | ~97,000               |                                 |
| Australia      | ~86,000               | Growing ~11.4%                  |

Russia was also a major market early in the year.
