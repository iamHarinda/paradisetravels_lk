## Money

The currency is the **Sri Lankan Rupee (LKR)**. Cards are widely accepted in cities and hotels; carry cash for small shops and tuk-tuks.

## Visiting temples

- Cover shoulders and knees.
- Remove shoes and hats before entering.
- Never pose for a photo with your back to a Buddha statue.

## Poya days

Every full moon (Poya) is a public holiday. Alcohol sales are restricted, and temples are busy with worshippers dressed in white.

## On the road

Driving is on the left. Most visitors hire a car with a driver — see [how to travel around Sri Lanka](/travel-guide/how-to-travel-around-sri-lanka).

## Emergency numbers

- Police: **119**
- Ambulance (Suwa Seriya): **1990**
- Tourist hotline: **1912** — Sri Lanka Tourism's information line from any phone in Sri Lanka; it can also connect you to the Tourism Police for complaints such as harassment.
