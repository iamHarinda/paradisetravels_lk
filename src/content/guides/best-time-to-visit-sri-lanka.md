**The short answer:** Sri Lanka is always in season somewhere. Two monsoons hit the island at different times of year, so when one coast is wet, the other is usually dry.

## The two main seasons
| Region | Best time (roughly) | Rain (roughly) |
|---|---|---|
| West & south coasts (Galle, Mirissa, Bentota) | December–April | May–September (south-west monsoon) |
| Hill country (Kandy, Nuwara Eliya, Ella) | December–April | May–September |
| East coast (Trincomalee, Nilaveli, Arugam Bay) | April/May–September | October–January |
| Cultural Triangle (Sigiriya, Polonnaruwa) | Much of the year | October–January (north-east monsoon); hottest March–May |

## Seasonal highlights
- **Whale watching:** Mirissa roughly November–April; Trincomalee roughly May–October.
- **Surfing:** Arugam Bay roughly May–October.
- **Kandy Esala Perahera:** July/August — exact dates follow the lunar calendar and are announced each year.

## Busiest months
December to March is peak season, with record visitor numbers in January and February 2026. Book hotels and train seats early for these months.
